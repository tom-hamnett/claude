# Game coach: sources

None of the practitioner books were read in full. Each entry says what was actually used. "Summary" means third-party summaries or reviews; "known concept" means a widely documented community term whose meaning is consistent across many write-ups (Wikipedia, review sites, forum glossaries). Research entries marked "rubric [n]" are already documented in `recorder/rubrics/social_confidence.md` section 6 (which has the full citation and what was checked); they are re-used here, not re-read.

Credibility shorthand: **P** practitioner (no controlled testing), **R** peer-reviewed research, **C** critique/analysis of the seduction community.

## A. Practitioner books and programmes (1-35)

1. Strauss, N. (2005). *The Game: Penetrating the Secret Society of Pickup Artists*. ReganBooks. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_(Strauss_book). Used: Wikipedia, review summaries, known concepts (openers, DHV, negs, IOIs, "AMOGing"). **P**, memoir; documents the community more than it validates it.
2. Strauss, N. (2007). *Rules of the Game* (Stylelife Challenge + The Style Diaries). St. Martin's. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rules_of_the_Game_(book). Used: Wikipedia, 2007 News24 coverage (https://www.news24.com/life/relationships/dating/seeking-love-prepare-well-20071129). **P**; 30-day exposure programme; drop-out figures are the author's own.
3. Strauss, N. (2015). *The Truth: An Uncomfortable Book About Relationships*. Dey Street. Used: book notes https://grahammann.net/book-notes/the-truth-neil-strauss, Blinkist summary https://www.blinkist.com/en/books/the-truth-en. **P**, memoir; useful as Strauss's own revision of PUA (intimacy, childhood patterns).
4. von Markovik, E. ("Mystery") & Odom, C. (2007). *The Mystery Method: How to Get Beautiful Women Into Bed*. St. Martin's. Used: description of the A-C-S three-phase model in https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10429087, Wikipedia, known concepts (groups, openers, DHV, IOIs, false time constraint, negs, kino). **P**; claims evolutionary basis, untested.
5. DeAngelo, D. (pen name of Eben Pagan) (c. 2002). *Double Your Dating* (e-book and programmes). Used: review https://thepowermoves.com/?p=128106, Lulu listing, known concepts (cocky-funny, "attraction isn't a choice", don't be a "nice guy"). **P**; influential origin of cocky-funny.
6. Deida, D. (1997; 20th anniv. ed. 2017). *The Way of the Superior Man*. Sounds True. Used: summary https://www.shortform.com/summary/the-way-of-the-superior-man-summary-david-deida, publisher blurb. **P**, spiritual; polarity claims are untested and gender-essentialist.
7. Wayne, C. (2006). *How to Be a 3% Man*. Self-published. Used: Blinkist https://www.blinkist.com/en/books/how-to-be-a-3-percent-man-en, Shortform https://www.shortform.com/pdf/how-to-be-a-3-percent-man-pdf-corey-wayne, his YouTube coaching. **P**.
8. Glover, R. (2003). *No More Mr Nice Guy*. Running Press. Used: Goodreads reader notes on covert contracts https://www.goodreads.com/notes/11755920-no-more-mr-nice-guy/64283789-nick/dc436d6e-6fe2-4d0c-ab62-1d295a061131, author site drglover.com, known concepts. **P** (therapist-author); ideas overlap with assertiveness and people-pleasing literature.
9. Manson, M. (2011). *Models: Attract Women Through Honesty*. Used: https://grahammann.net/book-notes/models-mark-manson, https://www.shortform.com/blog/models-book-mark-manson/. **P**; explicitly anti-manipulation.
10. Manson, M. (2016). *The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F\*ck*. HarperOne; plus markmanson.net dating articles. Used: general summaries and author site. **P**.
11. Cooper, R. (2020). *The Unplugged Alpha*. Used: review https://thepowermoves.com/the-unplugged-alpha-summary-review/, Shortform teaser https://www.shortform.com/pdf/the-unplugged-alpha-pdf-richard-cooper, Audible listing. **P**, red-pill; ideology marked contested; only frame/self-investment behaviours kept.
12. Tomassi, R. (2013-2023). *The Rational Male* series (incl. *Preventive Medicine*, *Positive Masculinity*, *The Players Handbook*). Used: author Substack https://rationalmale.substack.com/p/what-is-game, Goodreads listings and reviews, known concepts (frame, "mental point of origin", dread). **P**, red-pill; hypergamy claims contested.
13. Greene, R. (2001). *The Art of Seduction*. Viking/Penguin. Used: https://www.grahammann.net/book-notes/the-art-of-seduction-robert-greene, Shortform and SuperSummary overviews. **P**, historical anecdote; several steps are explicitly manipulative.
14. Judge, R. *The Light Switch*. **Could not be verified**: no listing found in searches of the web, his site or retailers. Used instead: Rob Judge's site https://robjudge.net (posts on statements creating attraction, unpredictability, neediness; four aspects Drive/Inspiration/Connection/Logistics on https://robjudge.net/about-rob/). Ask Tom for the edition or link. **P**.
15. Judge, R. & Bauer, Z. *The 4 Elements of Game*. https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/16060032. Used: listing description (attraction as tension and release). **P**.
16. South, J. W., Clare, D. & "Franco" (Lulu, 2019 ed.). *Practical Female Psychology: For the Practical Man*. Used: retailer and library listings (Audible, Goodreads, Bookmate, Alibris). **Author check:** listings credit Joseph W. South, David Clare and Franco, not "Kevin Garcia". Content not verifiable beyond blurbs; used only for generic, consensus points. **P**, low credibility.
17. "Pook" (forum posts, early 2000s; compiled as *The Book of Pook*). Used: compilation listings (https://jacknapier368.gumroad.com/l/nuipz, Kobo), known concepts (put yourself first, women as part of a full life, don't put her on a pedestal). Real author unverified. **P**.
18. SoSuave Discussion Forum (sosuave.com/forum, since late 1990s). Used: general community glossary and recurring advice ("AFC", "next", don't pedestalize, plenty of fish). **P**, anonymous forum; consensus at best.
19. Rowland, I. (2000, 3rd ed.). *The Full Facts Book of Cold Reading*. Used: SMU teaching page https://www.physics.smu.edu/pseudo/ColdReading/methods.html. **P** (mentalist); explains why cold reads feel accurate (Barnum effect [81]).
20. Jeffries, R. (1992). *How to Get the Women You Desire Into Bed*; "Speed Seduction" programmes. Used: secondary write-ups; known concepts (patterning, embedded commands, anchoring). **P**; NLP-based, see [80]; largely manipulative in intent.
21. Houpert, C. (2014). *Charisma on Command*; YouTube channel. Used: summary https://sobrief-staging.onrender.com/books/charisma-on-command, channel content. **P**.
22. Cabane, O. F. (2012). *The Charisma Myth*. Portfolio. Rubric [62]. Presence-power-warmth model. **P**; borrows from research but framework untested.
23. Savoy, N. / Love Systems (2007). *Magic Bullets*; Love Systems *Routines Manual*. Used: press release https://www.newswire.com/view/content/love-systems-inc-releases-new-book-magic-bullets-how-to-attract-a-girl-99106, Routines Manual chapter structure (Emotional Progression Model: attraction, qualification, comfort). **P**; success-rate claims unverified.
24. Cook, O. ("Tyler") / Real Social Dynamics (RSD) (2000s-2010s); *The Blueprint Decoded* (2016). Used: https://metropolisjapan.com/real-social-dynamics/, academic account of the 2014 Julien Blanc controversy https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/document/796327, known concepts (state, "vibe", reference point, outcome independence). **P**; company associated with documented coercive teaching (Blanc).
25. Kinrys, M. *The Wing Girl Method* / *Ask Women* podcast (wingirlmethod.com). Used: author site and podcast topics as recalled; not verified by search in this session. **P**, female perspective.
26. Hussey, M. (2013). *Get the Guy*. Harper Wave; howtogettheguy.com and YouTube. Used: author site/channel. **P**, female-perspective coach (mainly to women; useful for what women say they want).
27. Perel, E. (2006). *Mating in Captivity*. Harper. Used: https://www.themarginalian.org/2016/10/13/mating-in-captivity-esther-perel/, SuperSummary themes. **P** (couples therapist); clinical observation, not trials.
28. Lowndes, L. (2003). *How to Talk to Anyone*. McGraw-Hill. Used: general summaries. **P**.
29. Van Edwards, V. (2017). *Captivate*. Portfolio. Used: author site scienceofpeople.com, summaries. **P** with research citations.
30. Van Edwards, V. (2022). *Cues*. Portfolio. Used: author site, summaries (warmth + competence cues, vocal cues). **P** with research citations.
31. The Art of Charm podcast (Jordan Harbinger and AJ Harbinger, later AJ Harbinger & Johnny Dzubak). artofcharm.com. Used: podcast topics. **P**.
32. Voss, C. (2016). *Never Split the Difference*. Harper Business. Used: summaries (mirroring, labelling, calibrated questions, late-night FM DJ voice). **P** (negotiation); labelling has research support [82].
33. Johnstone, K. (1979). *Impro: Improvisation and the Theatre*. Faber. Used: summaries of status games and "yes-and". **P** (theatre).
34. Carnegie, D. (1936). *How to Win Friends and Influence People*. Used: general knowledge of principles (interest in others, names). **P**.
35. Bandler, R. & Grinder, J. (1979). *Frogs into Princes* and NLP generally. Used: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuro-linguistic_programming and review [80]. **P**; core claims not supported.

## B. Critiques and analyses of the seduction community (36-40)

36. Hambling-Jones, O. & Merrison, A. (2012). Inequity in the pursuit of intimacy: An analysis of British pick-up artist interactions. *Journal of Pragmatics*, 44(9), 1115-1127. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pragma.2012.04.008. Used: abstract. **C**; conversation analysis of real (covertly recorded) PUA talk; frames tactics as risky im/politeness plays.
37. Scotto di Carlo, G. (2023). "No does not always mean no": The discursive representation of female sexual rejection as "last minute resistance" in pick-up artist communities. *International Journal of Communication*. https://ijoc.org/index.php/ijoc/article/view/20283. Used: abstract. **C**.
38. Hall, J. A. & Canterberry, M. (2011). Sexism and assertive courtship strategies. *Sex Roles*. Press summary: https://news.ku.edu/2011/09/13/pickup-artist-strategies-appeal-dangerous-combo-sexist-couples-ku-researchers-say. **R/C**; PUA-style strategies were preferred mainly by sexist men and appealed mainly to sexist women; sexist attitudes linked to coercion.
39. Wikipedia: Seduction community. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seduction_community. Used: overview and glossary cross-check. Tertiary.
40. Social confidence rubric (this repo): `recorder/rubrics/social_confidence.md`. Myth-vs-evidence table and D1-D11.
