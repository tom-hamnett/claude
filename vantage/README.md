## 1. The problem, and why this works

We don't lack communication *knowledge* — books, courses and classrooms are everywhere. What's missing is feedback on **what you actually do** in real conversations. Academia names this precisely: leadership training is genuinely effective on learning (d = .73) but suffers a well-documented **"transfer problem"** — targeted behaviours fail to show up back in the real workplace (Lacerenza et al., 2017, meta-analysis of 335 samples; transfer d = .82 *when* transfer is supported). **VANTAGE is the transfer layer**: it teaches the skill *and* measures whether it actually appears in your real meetings.

This resolves the dominant pain — the **Feedback Fog**: people are told they "lack executive presence/gravitas" but never told, specifically and observably, what to *do*. VANTAGE turns vague feedback into named, measured, practiseable behaviours tied to specific modules.

## 2. The convergence insight (why one curriculum works)

Across domains the *same* core mechanics recur — regulate yourself, listen deeply, get curious, find the real need beneath the position, speak with clarity and intention. "Interests not positions" (principled negotiation), "tactical empathy / calibrated questions" (Voss), SPIN's "need-payoff" questions (consultative selling), and Sofer's "needs beneath positions" are the *same skill*. That shared spine is VANTAGE's intellectual backbone.

**Positioning:** *"Executive presence isn't a personality you're born with or a code you're locked out of. It's a set of specific, observable behaviours — and now you can see yours on the tape, and train them."*

## 3. Intellectual & assessment foundations

What we *teach* and what we *measure* use the same vocabulary:

- **Bates ExPI** — the only scientifically validated executive-presence model (10 PhD experts; 100+ leaders; multi-rater; strong content validity + reliability). Three dimensions, 15 facets:
  - **Character:** Authenticity, Integrity, Concern, Restraint, Humility.
  - **Substance:** Practical Wisdom, Confidence, Composure, Resonance, Vision.
  - **Style:** Appearance, Intentionality, Inclusiveness, Interactivity, Assertiveness.
- **Hewlett / CTI** — executive presence = gravitas (67%) + communication (28%) + appearance (5%); "know your stuff cold / six questions deep."
- **Spitzberg & Cupach communication competence** — the measurable behavioural layer: **Attentiveness**, **Composure**, **Coordination**, **Expressiveness**.
- **Say What You Mean (Sofer)** — the internal "how" spine in every module: the choice-point/pause, intention (curiosity & care), deep listening, observation-over-evaluation, needs beneath positions.

Rubric built as **behaviourally anchored rating scales (BARS)** (cf. RUCIS; McCroskey SPCC). Outcomes tracked on **Kirkpatrick** levels (reactions → learning → transfer → results), transfer as headline.

## 4. Curriculum architecture — 14 modules, 4 tracks

Each module is **non-linear and referenceable** (no mandatory order) and ships with **teaching material** (concept, why it matters, the internal mechanic, BARS-anchored good/poor examples) + **practice material** (real-world drills, in-meeting experiments, reflection, and self-recording challenges that feed the diagnostic). A **Socratic AI tutor** lets users interrogate any concept on demand. *(No AI role-play "practice partner".)*

## 5. The AI diagnostic (holistic, self-only evaluation)

Upload a real conversation. The AI reads the **whole conversation** for context, tone and how the situation unfolded, then assesses **only the user** across the competency framework — it speaks to *how you handled the situation*, never rating the other participants. Output is a **dashboard, not a number**:
- strengths vs. focus-this-week, with trend vs. history;
- specific quoted evidence both ways (strong moments + growth moments with a concrete alternative);
- a "how you handled situations" narrative;
- one focus + **direct links into the relevant modules**;
- longitudinal competency trends (the transfer metric, made visible).

**The loop:** diagnose → modules/practice → do it in a real conversation → re-upload → see movement.

## 6. Privacy & anti-bias (non-negotiable)
- **Self-only evaluation, full-conversation context** — *"We coach you, not the room."* Others' speech used transiently for context; not surfaced/stored as judgments. Encryption; user-set retention + one-tap delete.
- **Anti-bias:** score effectiveness toward the user's *own* goal and chosen style; never penalise accent or reward raw talk-time/extroversion; user sets a "this is me" baseline; transparent rubric (make the invisible rules visible).
