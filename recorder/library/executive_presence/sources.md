# Executive Presence library: sources

Version 1.0, 23 Oct 2026. Numbers are the `ref` values used in `techniques.json` and `taxonomy.md`. They are **not** the same as the numbers in `recorder/rubrics/executive_presence.md`; source [64] is that rubric, and research it cites is re-listed here where a technique relies on it.

**What "used" means.** None of the books were read in full. "Summary" = third-party book summaries or reviews (getAbstract, Shortform, Blinkist, Readingraphics, Goodreads reader notes, Publishers Weekly); "author material" = the author's own site, talk, podcast or interview; "abstract" = journal abstract or university press release; "rubric" = already checked for the rubric and taken from there. All technique text is paraphrased; no passages are reproduced.

**Credibility key.** Research = peer-reviewed empirical work. Practitioner = experienced coach/author, evidence mainly case-based. Grading in `techniques.json`: *research-backed* where at least one study tests the behaviour or its direct mechanism; *practitioner-consensus* where two or more independent practitioner sources teach it; *single-author* where only one source does; *contested* where research conflicts or failed to replicate.
