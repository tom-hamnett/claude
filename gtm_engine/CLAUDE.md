### LAYER 3 — Master Asset and Derivative Content Factory

This is the core creative engine. Every piece of content begins as a MASTER ASSET
and is then automatically derived into every relevant channel-specific format.

#### 3A — Master Asset Generation

A master asset is the fully developed idea: the insight, the argument, the narrative,
the data point or the story — written to its fullest expression without channel constraints.
It contains:
- The core insight or finding
- The uncomfortable truth it surfaces
- The evidence or reasoning behind it
- The point of view the brand takes
- The emotional arc (hook, tension, resolution, CTA)

Master assets are always generated by Claude. They are the source of truth for all derivatives.

#### 3B — Derivative Pipeline

From every master asset, the engine automatically generates ALL of the following:

FORMAT: Long-form article | CHANNEL: Substack, Blog | AI: Claude | NOTES: 800-1500 words, full narrative
FORMAT: LinkedIn article | CHANNEL: LinkedIn | AI: Claude | NOTES: Condensed, professional framing
FORMAT: LinkedIn carousel | CHANNEL: LinkedIn | AI: GPT-4o + design prompt | NOTES: 6-10 slide arc
FORMAT: Short post x3 | CHANNEL: LinkedIn, X/Twitter | AI: GPT-4o | NOTES: Three angle variations
FORMAT: Reel/TikTok script | CHANNEL: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | AI: Claude | NOTES: 45-75 sec
FORMAT: Talking head script | CHANNEL: YouTube, LinkedIn Video | AI: Claude | NOTES: 3-5 min expert style
FORMAT: Email broadcast | CHANNEL: Subscribers | AI: Claude | NOTES: Established lists only (500+)
FORMAT: Email lifecycle | CHANNEL: Triggered sequences | AI: Claude | NOTES: See Layer 4 audience strategy
FORMAT: Reddit post | CHANNEL: Relevant subreddits | AI: Claude | NOTES: Community-first, zero pitch
FORMAT: Visual brief | CHANNEL: Instagram static, LinkedIn image | AI: Gemini | NOTES: Art direction output
FORMAT: Video production brief | CHANNEL: All video formats | AI: Gemini | NOTES: Scene structure, B-roll

Default behaviour: generate ALL derivatives from every master asset.
Channel prioritisation determines sequencing and boost decisions — not whether to deploy.

#### 3C — Multi-AI Routing Logic

CLAUDE handles:
- Master asset generation (always)
- Long-form articles and Substack posts
- Email sequences (lifecycle and broadcast)
- Reel and talking head scripts
- Reddit posts (community tone requires nuance)
- Strategic content requiring reasoning chains

GPT-4o handles:
- High-volume social copy variation
- LinkedIn carousel text
- A/B headline and hook testing
- Short punchy post generation at scale

GEMINI handles:
- SEO-optimised blog content
- YouTube scripts (search-intent aware)
- Visual and video production briefs
- Multimodal content direction
- Research synthesis from web sources

FLUX / DALL-E / SORA handles:
- Static image generation from visual briefs
- Thumbnail generation
- Short cinematic video clips (Sora when available)
- Brand visual consistency checks

#### 3D — Content Tagging System

Every derivative is tagged with:
- master_asset_id: links back to source
- format: reel_script / linkedin_post / email_lifecycle / etc.
- channel: deployment destination
- segment: target customer segment
- strategic_objective: awareness / trust / conversion / retention
- edginess_principle: which of the 5 principles it primarily applies
- ai_model: which model generated it
- audience_stage: cold / warm / subscriber / customer
- boost_recommended: true/false with budget suggestion
- urgency: standard / priority / breaking

#### 3E — Content Type Pipelines

Each content type follows a defined multi-step AI workflow. These are standard operating
procedures the engine follows every time — not suggestions.

PIPELINE: Short-Form Video (Reel / TikTok / YouTube Short)

  Step 1 — THEME EXTRACTION (Claude)
    Input: master_asset.json
    Task: Extract the single most compelling hook
    Output: hook_options.json (3 variations, ranked)

  Step 2 — SCRIPT GENERATION (Claude)
    Input: selected hook + master asset
    Format: [HOOK 0-5s] [TENSION 5-20s] [INSIGHT 20-45s] [CTA 45-60s]
    Output: reel_script.md with pause markers and emphasis notes

  Step 3 — VISUAL BRIEF (Gemini)
    Input: reel_script.md
    Task: Scene-by-scene visual direction
    Output: visual_brief.md (on-screen text, B-roll, transitions)

  Step 4 — PRODUCTION BRIEF (Claude)
    Input: reel_script.md + visual_brief.md
    Output: production_brief.md ready for founder recording or AI avatar

  Step 5 — BENCHMARK CHECK (Claude)
    Input: production_brief.md + brand_standards.json
    Output: approved / revision_needed with specific notes

  Step 6 — QUEUE (System)
    Output: queued in content_queue with all tags


PIPELINE: Long-Form Article (Substack / Blog)

  Step 1 — NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE (Claude)
    Input: master_asset.json
    Output: article_outline.md (hook, tension, evidence, resolution, CTA)

  Step 2 — FULL DRAFT (Claude)
    Input: article_outline.md + brand_standards.json
    Output: article_draft.md (800-1500 words)

  Step 3 — SEO LAYER (Gemini) — blog/website version only
    Input: article_draft.md
    Output: article_seo.md with optimised title, meta, keywords

  Step 4 — BENCHMARK CHECK (Claude)
    Output: approved / revision_needed

  Step 5 — DERIVATIVE TRIGGER (System)
    Task: Automatically queue all other format derivatives
    Output: derivative jobs queued in content_factory


PIPELINE: Expert Interview / Talking Head Video

  Step 1 — STORY ARCHITECTURE (Claude)
    Format: [COLD OPEN 0-20s] [CONTEXT 20-60s] [3 KEY INSIGHTS 60-240s] [CLOSE + CTA]
    Output: video_narrative.md

  Step 2 — FULL SCRIPT (Claude)
    Output: video_script.md (word-for-word with presenter direction)

  Step 3 — B-ROLL AND VISUAL DIRECTION (Gemini)
    Output: visual_direction.md (scenes, data visualisations, graphics)

  Step 4 — VOICEOVER BRIEF (Claude)
    Output: voiceover_brief.md (tone, emphasis, pacing)

  Step 5 — PRODUCTION PACKAGE (Claude)
    Output: production_package/ folder with all components


PIPELINE: Email Lifecycle Sequence

  Step 1 — SEQUENCE ARCHITECTURE (Claude)
    Input: gtm_strategy.json + subscriber_stage
    Output: sequence_map.json (number of emails, timing, objective per email)

  Step 2 — INDIVIDUAL EMAIL GENERATION (Claude)
    Format per email: Subject line x3 variants + preview text + body + CTA
    Output: email_sequence/ folder

  Step 3 — PERSONALISATION HOOKS (GPT-4o)
    Output: email_sequence_personalised/ with segment variants

  Step 4 — DELIVERABILITY CHECK (System)
    Output: deliverability_report.md

  Step 5 — LOAD TO ESP (System)
    Output: sequence live in SendGrid/Resend as triggered automation


PIPELINE: Reddit / Community Post

  Step 1 — COMMUNITY AUDIT (Claude)
    Input: target subreddit + community rules
    Output: community_brief.md (tone, what performs, what gets removed)

  Step 2 — VALUE-FIRST FRAMING (Claude)
    Input: master_asset.json + community_brief.md
    Output: reddit_post_draft.md (zero pitch, genuine contribution)

  Step 3 — AUTHENTICITY CHECK (Claude)
    Task: Remove any trace of promotional intent
    Output: approved / revision_needed

  Step 4 — TIMING AND SUBREDDIT SELECTION (System)
    Output: queued at peak engagement time for selected subreddits

### LAYER 4 — Audience Development Module

Each channel has a fundamentally different growth mechanic.
The engine maintains a separate playbook per channel and adapts
content strategy based on current audience size and growth stage.

REDDIT / SEO:
  Growth mechanic: Algorithmic. Zero audience needed. Works day one.
  Strategy: Lead with genuine value. Never pitch. Build karma first.
  Sequence: 10 value-add comments before first post. Monitor karma velocity.
  SEO parallel: Every blog post targets a long-tail search query. Gemini optimises.
  Metric: Upvotes, comment engagement, traffic to owned properties

LINKEDIN:
  Growth mechanic: Network-compounding. Consistent posting drives follower growth.
  Strategy: 3x per week minimum. Mix short posts and long-form articles.
  Connection strategy: Automated but personalised outreach to target segment profiles.
  Metric: Follower growth rate, post impressions, profile visits, connection acceptance rate

EMAIL:
  Growth mechanic: Lifecycle-triggered sequences, NOT batch sends to small lists
  Rule: Never send a broadcast until list exceeds 500 engaged subscribers
  Before 500: All email is triggered lifecycle sequences based on subscriber actions
  Lifecycle sequence:
    Day 0: Welcome + strongest content piece
    Day 3: The uncomfortable truth post (category punching)
    Day 7: Product demonstration / proof of concept
    Day 14: Social proof + case study
    Day 21: Soft conversion offer
    Ongoing: Weekly value email triggered by content calendar
  List building: Lead magnet per product (free diagnostic sample / free Atlas methodology guide)
  Metric: Open rate, click rate, sequence completion rate, conversion rate

INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK:
  Growth mechanic: Low organic reach without paid boost
  Strategy: Every Reel gets a boost decision score 0-10 based on quality and segment fit
  Boost threshold: Score 7+ gets $20-50 boost
  Budget logic: Monthly Instagram budget divided by estimated posts = per-post ceiling
  Targeting: Lookalike audiences from email list + interest targeting per segment
  Metric: Reach, saves (highest intent signal), profile visits, link clicks, follower growth

SUBSTACK:
  Growth mechanic: Substack recommendations engine + SEO + cross-promotion
  Strategy: Publish weekly. Every post optimised for Substack search.
  Growth lever: Recommend relevant Substacks, request recommendations in return
  Cross-promotion: Every post promoted on LinkedIn and Reddit
  Metric: Subscriber growth, open rate, paid conversion rate

X / TWITTER:
  Growth mechanic: Retweet velocity + reply engagement
  Strategy: Atlas account posts daily. Market observations, methodology transparency, data.
  Thread format performs best for this audience.
  Metric: Follower growth, retweet rate, reply quality (are the right people engaging?)

### LAYER 6 — Live Intelligence Feed

Always-open founder signal input. Can be triggered at any time — not just at stage gates.

What triggers a live intelligence injection:
- A surprising customer conversation or quote
- A new data point or analysis finding
- A competitor move worth commenting on
- A market event creating a content opportunity
- A new use case or product insight
- A personal observation or contrarian view

How it works:

  Step 1 — FOUNDER INPUT
    Raw input accepted in any format: text, paste, voice transcript, data table
    No structure required — engine extracts the signal

  Step 2 — SIGNAL ASSESSMENT (Claude)
    Assess significance, what it changes or confirms
    Score: Priority level 1-5 and content opportunity rating
    Output: signal_assessment.json

  Step 3 — CONTENT BRIEF GENERATION (Claude)
    Priority 5: Full master asset + all derivatives immediately
    Priority 3-4: Master asset + top 3 derivatives
    Priority 1-2: Single best-fit derivative, queued normally
    Output: content_brief.json with recommended derivative set

  Step 4 — FOUNDER CONFIRMATION
    Present content brief and recommended derivatives
    One-click approval to queue everything

  Step 5 — QUEUE WITH URGENCY TAG
    Breaking content can displace scheduled posts if warranted

### LAYER 7 — Stage Gate and Reporting Engine

- Default: weekly trigger. Also fires on: performance drop, content diversity decay,
  cadence drop, or engine detecting repetitive content patterns

Stage gate challenge questions:
- What has happened in your business since last week?
- Have any customers said anything surprising or unexpected?
- What do you believe about your market now that you did not believe before?
- Is there anything you are seeing that contradicts what we are currently doing?
- What is the most uncomfortable truth about your product right now?
- Has anything happened that should change our channel priorities?

Answers are injected as fresh context into the content brain, potential Live Intelligence
Feed triggers, and strategy assumption updates logged with reasoning.

Weekly report covers:
1. WHAT RAN — all content deployed by channel, format, segment
2. WHAT PERFORMED — top and bottom performers with attribution
3. WHAT CHANGED — reprioritisation decisions and reasoning
4. AUDIENCE GROWTH — follower/subscriber/list metrics vs previous week
5. WHAT IS NEXT — upcoming queue, boost recommendations, stage gate actions needed

---

### LAYER 8 — Brand and Standards Module

Configured once during setup. Referenced at every content benchmark check.
Sits above all content generation as the permanent guardrail layer.

brand_standards.json contains:

VOICE:
  - Tone descriptors (e.g. sharp, transparent, authoritative, never corporate)
  - Forbidden phrases (e.g. game-changer, revolutionary, unlock your potential)
  - Vocabulary level: write up, assume intelligent reader
  - Sentence style: short and punchy by default, considered for long-form

EDGINESS CALIBRATION:
  - Edginess level 1-10 (set per brand, overridable per piece)
  - Which edginess principles to weight most heavily
  - Category norms this brand actively challenges
  - Topics requiring approval before publication

VISUAL STANDARDS:
  - Brand colour palette (hex codes)
  - Typography preferences
  - Image style direction (photography vs illustration vs data visualisation)
  - Video aesthetic (talking head style, B-roll preferences, on-screen text style)

CONTENT STANDARDS:
  - Minimum quality threshold per format
  - CTA standards per channel
  - Disclosure requirements

APPROVAL WORKFLOW:
  - Auto-approve: content above quality threshold passing benchmark check
  - Flag for review: content below threshold, high-stakes channels, boosted posts
  - Always require approval: named companies, legal or financial claims

## CODING STANDARDS

- Every module has a clear single responsibility
- Every function has a docstring explaining what it does and what it returns
- All API calls wrapped in retry logic with exponential backoff
- All outputs saved to disk as JSON and/or Markdown with timestamps
- Every strategic or reprioritisation decision logged with timestamp and plain English reasoning
- Environment variables only — no hardcoded keys ever
- Modular design — each layer can run independently or as part of the full pipeline
- Comments explain WHY not just WHAT
- Every pipeline step saves intermediate outputs — never lose work to a failed API call
